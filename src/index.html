About Me

I started in customer support at AttractWell and GetOiling in 2020 and never stopped following problems to their source. Today I diagnose issues across PHP, MySQL, DNS and third-party APIs, ship fixes through reviewed pull requests, and build AI agents that do the investigation work I used to do by hand.

I work async on purpose. Written work leaves a trail, scales across time zones, and forces clear thinking, and over 1,400 tickets this year show it works. I’m based in BC’s Okanagan.
